Introduction

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT), formerly known as Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM), is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. The university was officially chartered on 1 February 2007 and has become one of Malaysia's leading institutions in marine science and aquatic resource studies.

UMT originated from Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science, which was established to provide facilities for teaching, practical training, and research activities. Over time, the institution expanded and evolved into a fully-fledged university with various academic programs and research initiatives.

Today, UMT continues to contribute to higher education and scientific research, particularly in marine sciences, environmental sustainability, and technology. The university attracts students from different regions and promotes innovation through teaching, research, and community engagement.
