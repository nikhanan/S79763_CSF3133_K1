About the Festival

Taste of Terengganu brings together the best local food stalls for three days of eating, music and culture by the river. Visitors can enjoy famous dishes such as nasi dagang, keropok lekor and laksam, prepared by local entrepreneurs and home cooks.

The festival also supports local businesses and students. Cooking demonstrations are held every evening and all stalls follow environmentally friendly practices.

Free entry for all UMT students – show your matric card at the entrance.
